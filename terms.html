The application is intended only for authorised administrators of the Pind Manak YouTube channel. It is not offered as a public service.

The application uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to perform authorised channel and live broadcast management functions.

Use of YouTube through this application is also subject to YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's applicable policies.

Authorisation

Only authorised Google accounts associated with the Pind Manak YouTube channel may grant the application access to YouTube data and management functions.
