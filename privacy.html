Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Pind Manak YouTube Automation, a private automation system used by authorised administrators of the Pind Manak YouTube channel.

Google and YouTube Data

The application uses Google OAuth and the YouTube Data API to access the Pind Manak YouTube account after permission has been granted by an authorised account holder.

The application may access and manage information required to operate the channel's live broadcasts, including broadcast details, schedules, stream configuration, titles, descriptions and thumbnails.

How Data Is Used

Google and YouTube data accessed by the application is used solely to operate and automate the Pind Manak YouTube channel and its live broadcasts.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Authentication

OAuth authentication credentials may be stored securely on the authorised computer running the automation so that the system can perform scheduled operations without requiring repeated sign-in.

Access

The application is not offered as a public service. Access is restricted to authorised administrators of the Pind Manak YouTube channel.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be sent to the support email shown on the application's Google OAuth consent screen.
